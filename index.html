One record for the whole school

  1. The School Head gives each department head designate the site’s address and the department heads’ access code. They enter the code once under Connection; after that they can add, change and delete entries.
  2. Everything saved here goes to one shared record, so the School Head sees every department’s plans, observations and coaching as soon as they are saved.
  3. Set the department of each teacher once in Personnel & Departments. The forms then fill in the department by themselves.
  4. Names and positions are picked from the PSIPOP list. For someone not yet on the PSIPOP, choose “Not in the list” and type the name and position.
  5. The Observation Notes Form can be printed straight from the site with Print Notes Form, or downloaded as a PDF or Word file. The COT Rating Sheet, Inter-Observer Agreement Form, Observation Notes Form and PMCF are filled into the original Annex templates, so they print exactly like the blank forms.
  6. In Classroom Observation, the Guide button on each indicator opens the Annex E-1 rubric for that indicator. Tick the features of practice you saw and the rating is set to the highest level ticked, within the teacher’s career-stage scale.
  7. In Monitoring & Coaching, the PMCF is one teacher per month. Guide: sample entries holds sample entries you can copy into the form and then edit.
  8. All dates are written in full, like October 14, 2026. Type a date in any common form (10/14/2026, Oct 14 2026) or pick it from the calendar; it is rewritten in full when you leave the field.

The supervision cycle and its annexes

Annex A
Teacher Self-Assessment (e-SAT). Teachers rate themselves on the PPST at the start of the year; the consolidation tool rolls up the school’s results to show where coaching is most needed.
Annex B
Monthly Instructional Supervisory Plan, prepared by each department head and approved by the School Head.
Annex C
PMES Tools for Teachers, one per career stage. The COT ratings saved here are the means of verification for the classroom-observation objectives.
Annex D
Performance Monitoring and Coaching Form. Every coaching session is logged as it happens.
Annex E
Classroom Observation Tool: full rubric (E-1), Rating Sheet (E-2), Inter-Observer Agreement Form (E-3) and Observation Notes Form (E-4).
Annex F
e-IPCRF and Midyear Review Form, where the year’s ratings are finally recorded.

How the COT scale is chosen

Levels 2–6
Beginning towards Proficient: Teacher I to Teacher III. Not Observed counts as 2.
Levels 3–7
Proficient: Teacher IV to Teacher VII. Not Observed counts as 3.
Levels 4–8
Highly Proficient: Master Teacher I and II. Not Observed counts as 4.
Levels 5–9
Distinguished: Master Teacher III to V. Not Observed counts as 5.
Indicators
The COT indicators change each school year: SY 2025–2026, SY 2026–2027 and SY 2027–2028 each have their own set, taken from the Annex E-2 rating sheets.

The average shown under the ratings is a quick reading for the observer. N/A indicators are left out of it; Not Observed counts at the lowest level of the scale.
